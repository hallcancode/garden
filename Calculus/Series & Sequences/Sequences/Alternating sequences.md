An *alternating sequence* is one who's terms alternate between signs, for an example:
$$
\sum^\infty_{n=1} \frac{\left(-1\right)^{n+1}}{n} = 1 - \frac12 + \frac13 - \frac14 + \dots
$$
Alternating sequences [[Convergence|converge]], if the absolute value of the sequence converges, that is, this is known as the *alternating series test*.
