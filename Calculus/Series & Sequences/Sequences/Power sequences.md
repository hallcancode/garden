Given a sequence of coefficients, $\left\{c_n\right\}$, a *power series* is a [[Introduction to Functions|function]] of the form:
$$
f\left(x\right) = \sum^\infty_{n=0} c_n x^n = c_0 x^0 + c_1 x^1+c_2x^2+\dots
$$
The [[Convergence|convergence]] of $f$ will then depend on the value of $x$, such that:
$$
\left|x-a\right| \lt R
$$
Additionally, it may or may not converge for $x=R$, where $R$ is the *radius of convergence* and $a$ is the *center of convergence*.  This interval of numbers is known as the *interval of convergence*.  Thus, a power series can be thought of as a set of infinite many infinite sets, where, for each value of $x$.
