*The ratio test* states that for a [[Infinite series|series]] with terms, $\sum a_n$, the limit of the absolute value of ratios between consecutive terms $L$, such that:
$$
L = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left| \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} \right|
$$
Then this limit of ratios gives the convergence of the original series, such that:
$$
\begin{align}
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| \lt 1 &\iff \sum{a_n} \ \text{is absolutely convergent.}\\

\lim_{n \to \infty} \left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| \gt 1 &\iff \sum{a_n} \ \text{is divergent.}\\
\end{align}
$$
If $L=1$, then it may diverge, converge absolutely, or converge conditionally.
