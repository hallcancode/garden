The $p$-series test is a fundamental tool for determining convergence and divergence of a special form of [[Infinite series|series]].  The $p$-series test states that, for any $p \in \mathbb{R}$.
$$
\sum^\infty_{n=1} \frac{1}{n^p}=
\begin{cases}
b&\text{for }p\gt1.\\
\text{diverges}&\text{for }p\le1.\\
\end{cases}
$$
Where $b \in \mathbb{R}$.
