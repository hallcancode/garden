A parametrization of a curve $\vec{r}\left(t\right)$ is called smooth if its [[Derivatives of Vector Functions|derivative]], $\mathrm{d}/\mathrm{d}t \space \vec{r}\left(t\right)$ matches the case:
$$
\left(\forall t \in \mathbb{R}\right)
\left(
	\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}
	\vec{r}\left(t\right) 
	\ne 0
\right)
\iff
\vec{r}\left(t\right)
\space

\text{is smooth throughout.}
$$
The curvature $\kappa$ of a curve $\mathrm{d}/\mathrm{d}t \space \vec{r}$ is defined to be the number:
$$
	\kappa = \left|\frac{\mathrm{d} \vec{T}}{\mathrm{d}s}\right|
$$
Where $\vec{T}$ is the tangent vector and $s$ is the [[Arc Length|arc length]].  The curvature should not depend upon the [[Parametrization|parametrization]].
