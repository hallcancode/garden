The [[Introduction to Integrals|integral]] of a vector-valued function is computed similar to the [[Derivatives of Vector Functions|derivative of a vector-valued function]], such that:
$$
\int_a^b \vec{r}\left(t\right) \cdot \mathrm{d} t =
\left\langle
\int_a^b r_x\left(t\right) \cdot \mathrm{d}t,
\int_a^b r_y\left(t\right) \cdot \mathrm{d}t,
\int_a^b r_z\left(t\right) \cdot \mathrm{d}t
\right\rangle
$$
Where $\vec{r}\left(t\right) = \left\langle r_x\left(t\right), r_y\left(t\right), r_z\left(t\right) \right\rangle$.
