A *quadric surface* is the graph of a second degree equation in the [[Introduction to Three Dimensional Systems|third dimension]], and can generally be summarized with two forms, such that:
$$
0=\begin{cases}
Ax^2 + By^2+Cz^2 + D \\
Ax^2 + By^2 + Cz + D
\end{cases}
$$
Quadric surfaces can be visualized with a process called *level curves*, where we fix a value (usually $z$) to a constant.  Then, we can trace the shape on an $\mathbb{R}^2$ plane.  This process is repeated for various constants of our fixed value.
