Limits are the value that a [[Introduction to Functions|function]] approaches as the input to that function approaches some number.  More generally, the limit $a$ of a function $f$ approaches as $x$ approaches some constant $k$ is written:
$$
\lim_{x \to k }f\left(x\right)=a
$$
Which is read “the limit as $x$ approaches $k$ of $f$ of $x$ is $a$.”
