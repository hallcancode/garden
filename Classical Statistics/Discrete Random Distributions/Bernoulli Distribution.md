A *Bernoulli distribution* is one where an outcome can be either $0$ or $1$.  It is one of the simplest distributions, modeling success or failure.  The probability mass function is given by the expression:
$$
P\left(X=x|\theta\right) = \theta^x \left(1-\theta\right)^{1-x}
$$
Where $X$ is the random variable, such that the fixed value $x \in \left\{0,1\right\}$, and the parameter value $\theta \in \left(0,1\right)$.
