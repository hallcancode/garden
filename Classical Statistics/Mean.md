The mean is a measurement of the "typical value" of a dataset. There are several different types of mean in math, each gives a summary of the magnitude, or overall size, and sign, can be positive, negative, or $0$, of a dataset.  The mean is also known as the expected value of a population.  Let $E$ be a function yielding an expected value and $a,b$ be some constants, suppose that $x,y$ are random variables, then:
1. $E\left(b\right)=b$
2. $E\left(aX\right) = a \cdot E\left(X\right)$

## Arithmetic mean
The arithmetic mean, often simply referred to as the mean, is the sum of the numbers divided by the total number of numbers. For a sample it is typically notated as $\bar{x}$ and $\mu$ for populations.  It can calculated like so:

$$
\bar{x}=\frac1n\sum^n_{i=1}x_i=\frac{x_1+x_2+\dots+x_n}{n}
$$
## Geometric mean
The geometric mean is the product of all numbers to the power of one over the total number of numbers.  It is useful for datasets that are all positive. It can be calculated with the following:

$$
\bar{x}=\left(\prod^n_{i=1}x_i\right)^\frac{1}{n}=\left(x_1x_2\dots{x_n}\right)^\frac{1}{n}
$$
