The variance of a population describes the spreading of values from the [[mean]].  Let $X \sim P\left(X=x\right)$, then we define the variance of $X$ as
$$
\text{var}\left(X\right) = \sum_x \left(x - \mu\right)^2 \cdot P\left(X=x\right)
$$
Where $\mu$ is the mean.  Variance has several properties, including:
1. $\text{var}\left(b\right) = 0$
2. $\text{var}\left(a X\right) = a^2 \text{var}\left(X\right)$
3. $\text{var}\left(a X + b\right) = a^2 \text{var}\left(X\right)$
4. $\text{var}\left(X + Y\right) = \text{var}\left(X\right) + \text{var}\left(Y\right)$, where $X,Y$ are two independent random variables.
