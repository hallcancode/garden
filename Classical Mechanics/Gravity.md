Gravity is the attraction between all particles.

## Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation
Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation describes that every particle as a being attracted to every other particle in this universe, thereby describing gravity as a force $F_g$: 

$$
F_g=G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2}
$$

Where $m_1$ and $m_2$ are the masses of the two particles, $r$ is the distance between both objects' center of masses, and $G$ is the gravitational constant.  

The gravitational constant is known to four significant figures, meaning that within SI units:

$$
G\approx{6.6743\times10^{-11}}\text{ m}^3\text{ kg}^{-1}\text{ s}^{-2}
$$

## Gravity on Earth
The magnitude of the acceleration due to gravity and gravitational field strength respectively, at Earth’s surface, $g$ is:

$$
\begin{aligned}
g&=9.8\text{ m}/\text{s}^2\\
 &=9.8\text{ N}/\text{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
