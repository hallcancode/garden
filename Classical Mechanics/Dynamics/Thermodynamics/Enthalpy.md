*Enthalpy*, $H$, is a state function that is the sum of a system's internal energy, $E$, and the product of the [[Pressure|pressure]], $P$, exerted upon it and its [[Volume|volume]], $V$, such that:
$$
\begin{align}
H &= E + PV
\end{align}
$$
Enthalpy is a state function, such that the change in enthalpy $\Delta H$ is equal to the difference in final and initial enthalpies, $H_f$ and $H_0$ respectively, such that:
$$
\Delta H = H_{f} - H_{0}
$$
Thus, change enthalpy may also be expressed as the difference in the quantities, and therefore, when under constant pressure, is equal to:
$$
\Delta H = \Delta E + W, \quad \Delta P=0.
$$
