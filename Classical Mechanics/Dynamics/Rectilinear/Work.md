Work $W$ is the amount of energy transferred to a moving object via a force excreted upon it along the object's displacement. Work is proportional to the force $F$ ad displacement $s$, such that:

$$
W=Fs\cos\left(\theta\right)
$$

Where $\theta$ is the angle at which the force is exerted.  Similarly, work is also the area under a force-displacement curve, such that:

$$
W=\int{F} ds
$$

## Work Done by Gravity
The work done by [[Gravity|gravity]] on Earth is proportional to the mass of the object $m$, the distance from Earth $h$, and the gravitational field strength $g$, such that:

$$
W=-mgh
$$
