The *factorial* of a number is defined as the product of all values preceding, and include, itself.  The factorial of some number, $n$, is defiend as the [[Introduction to Functions|recursive function]]:
$$
n! = \begin{cases}
1, & \text{For} \ n=0 \\
n \cdot \left(n-1\right)!, & \text{For} \  n\gt0 \\
\end{cases}
$$
