An *inverse function* is a special [[Introduction to Functions|function]] that maps the values of a function's codomain back to the values of its domain.  In other words, an inverse function allows us to "undo" a function.  By there very nature, inverse functions "flip" the codomain and domain.  Consider a function $f : X \to Y$ and its inverse:
$$
f^{-1} : Y \to X
$$
However, in order for $f$ to have an inverse, it must be a map from $X$ to $Y$.
