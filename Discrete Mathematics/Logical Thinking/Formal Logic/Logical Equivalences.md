Two [[Statement|logical statements]] are logically equivalent if they have the same true or false values for all cases.   

The _contrapositive_ of $p \to q$ is when the operands are negated and swapped to form the statement $\neg q \to \neg p$.  The form with just the operands swapped is called the _converse_, and in this case would be $q \to p$.  The contrapositive is logical equivalent, but the converse isn't.

| **$p$** | **$q$** | **$p\to q$** | **$q \to p$** | **$\neg q \to \neg p$** |
| :-: | :-: | :------: | :-------: | :-----------------: |
|  T  |  T  |    T     |     T     |          T          |
|  T  |  F  |    F     |     T     |          F          |
|  F  |  T  |    T     |     F     |          T          |
|  F  |  F  |    T     |     T     |          T          |
