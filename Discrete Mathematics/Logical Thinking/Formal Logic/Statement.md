_Statements_, also known as a _proposition_, are declarative sentences that are either "true" or "false," but never both.  There are two cases that cause a declarative sentence to fail to be a statement:

 - The sentence contains an unspecified term, or a "free variable," that the truth of the sentence depends on.  For an instance, the sentence "$x$ is even."
 - The sentence is self-referential, for an example, "this sentence is false."
