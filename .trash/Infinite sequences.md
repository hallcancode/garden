*Infinite sequences* are ordered lists of numbers that follow the pattern:
$$
\left\{a_n\right\}_{n=1}^\infty = \left\{a_1,a_2,a_3, \dots, a_n\right\}
$$
If this sequence gets close to some number as $n$ gets very large, then we say that the sequence is "convergent."  However, if the sequence is unbounded, then we say it is "divergent."
