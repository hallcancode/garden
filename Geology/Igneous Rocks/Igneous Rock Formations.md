*Batholiths* are the largest intrusion.  

*Sills* are planar intrusions that are parallel to sedimentary bedding.  They are much smaller than batholiths.  

*Dikes* are intrusions which cut across rock types, and are generally vertical and narrow.  They will cut across bedding in sedimentary rocks.  *Volcanic necks* had radiating dikes, a common example would be Shiprock, New Mexico.  

*Plutons* and *stocks* are smaller irregular or cylindrical intrusions.  They are larger than sills and dikes, but smaller than batholiths.
