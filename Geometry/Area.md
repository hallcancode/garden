Area is the amount of two-dimensional space that a shape takes up.  Area is typically measured in some unit squared (eg. m<sup>2</sup>, in<sup>2</sup>.)  

> [!Example 1]
> The area $A$ of a rectangle is proportional to its length $\ell$ and width $w$, such that:
> $$A=w\ell$$

> [!Example 2]
> The area $A$ of a circle is proportional to the radius of the circle $r$ and the constant Pi $\pi$, such that:
> $$A=\pi{r}^2$$
