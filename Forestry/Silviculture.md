*Silviculture* is the art and science of controlling the establishment, growth, composition, quality, and health of a [[Introduction to Forestry|forest]].  Silviculture following everything from planting and fertilization to harvesting and milling.  Four basic harvesting "styles:"

- **Clearcut**, artificially regenerated, Idaho FPA must be fully restocked within 5 years.
- **Seed Tree** cut, leaving a few remaining parent trees, about five to ten per an acre.
- **Shelterwood**, intended to "shelter" the site by planting new trees under old ones.
- **Selection**, individually select trees for harvest.

Remember, trees are a renewable natural resources.
