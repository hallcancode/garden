Everything that takes up space, [[Volume|volume]], and has a [[Mass|mass]] $m$, is considered to be matter.  

## Density
The property of matter that describes how tightly packed together the matter within a sample is density.  The density $\rho$ of an object with mass $m$ and volume $V$ is an inverse relationship, such that:
$$
\rho=\frac{m}{V}
$$
