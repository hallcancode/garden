The quantum numbers represent the quantum state of [[The Atom|electrons]].  Classically, there are four quantum numbers:

## Principal Quantum Number
The principal quantum number, $n$, represents which electron shell, or energy level, the electron is contained within.  The values of the principal number are natural numbers, such that:
$$n=1,2,3,\dots$$

## Azimuthal Quantum Number
The azimuthal quantum number, $\ell$, is also called the orbital angular momentum quantum number.  As the alternative name suggests, the azmuthal is related to the [[Angular Momentum|angular momentum]] such that:
$$
L^2 = \hbar^2 \ell (\ell + 1)
$$
Where $L$ is the angular momentum, $\hbar$ is the [[Planck Constant#Reduced Plank Constant|reduced Planck constant]], and $\ell$ is, of course, the azimuthal.  Due to the fact that the azimuthal describes the angular momentum of the orbit, it also describes the shape of the orbit.  Possible values for $\ell$ range from $0$ to $n-1$.
