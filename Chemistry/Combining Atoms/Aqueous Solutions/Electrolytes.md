When a substances conducts electricity within a [[Introduction to Combining Atoms#Homogenous Mixtures|solution]], it is called an electrolyte.  All electrolytes are good conductors of electricity, typically they are [[Bonding to Form Molecules & Compounds#Ionic Bonds|ionic compounds]] that are soluble in water.  In fact, electrolytes are the reason why water is a good conductor of electricity, by itself, as deionized water is not a good conductor of electricity.  

Strong electrolytes, such as particular acids, dissolve completely into the solution.
