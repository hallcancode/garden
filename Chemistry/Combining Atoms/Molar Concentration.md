Molar concentration is the measure of chemical concentration of some solute within a [[Introduction to Combining Atoms#Homogenous Mixtures|solution]].  Molarity $M$, or $c$, is defined as the number of [[The Atom|atoms]] (in moles) $n$ and volumes of the solution $V$ (in liters), such that:
$$
M=\frac{n}{V}
$$
## Serial & Diluted Concentration
The serial solution is the molar concentration of the stock solution, whereas the diluted concentration is the molar concentration after the serial solution has been diluted with some solute.  The serial and diluted concentrations are related via the serial dilution equation:
$$M_1V_1=M_2V_2$$
Where $M_1$ is the serial concentration, $V_1$ is the volume of the serial solution, $M_2$ is the diluted concentration, and $V_2$ is the volume of the diluted solution.
