
> “Fire has an important role, but we need to be careful with it.”

**Fire**, as a physical process, is also known as combustion.  It is an uninterrupted chain reaction between oxygen, energy (heat), and fuel.  Fire is the rapid decomposition of fuel.  Things that influence fire include:

 - Climate and local weather trends.  
 - Ecosystem (fuel).  
 - Geography (topography).  

Fire has both positive and negative effects, within:

 - Fire clears out old and dead plants, which is helpful for [[Introduction to Forestry|forested land]].  That said, it does also pose risks and cause setback in the management of forestry, which often takes years to manage.
 - Fire recycles nutrients within range lands.  
 - Fire makes rivers and streams more warm, causing harm to fish. 
 - Fire also causes more erosion which then falls into rivers, bring in nutrients, which at first overwhelm the ecosystems.  However, afterwards, there is a long-term positive effects.
